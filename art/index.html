Field notebook · Austin, Texas

Wildlife Illustration

I have always loved botanical illustration. Over a year spent in Austin, Texas, I photographed the flowers, plants, and small creatures I met along the way, then used generative models to turn those snapshots into a scrapbook of naturalist plates. Each card pairs an illustration with where I found it and the meaning traditionally attached to the bloom.

Floriography — the “language of flowers” — is symbolic and cultural rather than scientific, and its meanings vary by region and source.

Travel notebook · 2025–2026

Museum & Gallery Visits

The other half of this page. I love wandering museums and galleries, so over the past year I kept a little logbook of the ones I visited — nine collections across the States, pinned by the coordinates where each photo was taken. I have a soft spot for Monet, so his paintings are marked in gold.

  1. 01

    The Broad

    Los Angeles, CA · Jul 31, 2025 · 34.0544, −118.2507

    Contemporary and Pop under Diller Scofidio + Renfro's honeycomb “veil.”

  2. 02

    The Getty Center

    Los Angeles, CA · Aug 5, 2025 · 34.0780, −118.4741

    Travertine pavilions on a hill above the city — Old Masters and gardens.

  3. 03

    Art Institute of Chicago

    Chicago, IL · Oct 5, 2025 · 41.8796, −87.6237

    An Impressionist pilgrimage — Monet, Seurat, Van Gogh and Caillebotte all in one afternoon.

  4. 04

    Museum of Modern Art (MoMA)

    New York, NY · Dec 18, 2025 · 40.7614, −73.9776

    A modernist roll-call — from a starry night to a single field of blue.

  5. 05

    Carnegie Museum of Art

    Pittsburgh, PA · Jan 2, 2026 · 40.4436, −79.9492

    Turned a corner in Pittsburgh and found a whole wall of Monet's water lilies.

  6. 06

    Museum of Fine Arts, Boston

    Boston, MA · Jan 17, 2026 · 42.3394, −71.0942

    The Monet motherlode — five of his paintings in a single wing — plus a Sargent-domed rotunda and a lime-green tower of glass.

  7. 07

    Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum

    Boston, MA · Jan 21, 2026 · 42.3381, −71.0992

    Isabella's Venetian palazzo, built around a courtyard that blooms all year behind Gothic glass.

  8. 08

    Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History

    Washington, D.C. · Mar 21, 2026 · 38.8913, −77.0261

    A detour from art into deep time — the great rotunda, a totem hall, and the origins of us.

  9. 09

    George Peabody Library

    Baltimore, MD · Mar 23, 2026 · 39.2977, −76.6156

    Not a museum, but too beautiful to skip — a cathedral made of books.

Attributions are noted from memory and gallery labels; dates are the day of the visit. Coordinates come from each photo's location tag.
