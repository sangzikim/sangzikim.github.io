Field notebook · Austin, Texas

Wildlife Illustration

I have always loved botanical illustration. Over a year spent in Austin, Texas, I photographed the flowers, plants, and small creatures I met along the way, then used generative models to turn those snapshots into a scrapbook of naturalist plates. Each card pairs an illustration with where I found it and the meaning traditionally attached to the bloom.

Floriography — the “language of flowers” — is symbolic and cultural rather than scientific, and its meanings vary by region and source.
